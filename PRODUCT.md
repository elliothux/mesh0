# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers and platform engineers who build, operate, and scale large agent workloads. They are evaluating whether Mesh0 can give them a direct, programmable way to launch many agents, observe every run, and keep control over sandbox backends.

## Product Purpose

Mesh0 is open-source agent infrastructure for dispatching, observing, and managing agent fleets across sandbox backends. The website should make the core promise immediately clear: start with one SDK call, scale to thousands of tracked agent runs, and keep the stack self-hostable and extensible.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, high-performance. The voice should feel technical and confident, with terse copy, concrete API examples, and visible control-plane behavior.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI startup gloss, decorative chatbot imagery, cartoon mascots, vague productivity claims, oversized marketing cards, and dense enterprise dashboards. The page should not feel like an observability template or a crypto landing page.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the primitive: one call creates a fleet.
- Show the system working through code, events, artifacts, and backends.
- Make scale feel operationally controlled rather than theatrical.
- Keep copy terse and specific to developer infrastructure.
- Favor self-hostable confidence over black-box magic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-accessible controls. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Do not rely on color alone to communicate status or hierarchy.
